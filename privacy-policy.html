Privacy Policy

How PLASTICOS collects, uses and protects the information you share through this website.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by PLASTICOS – Advanced Hair Transplant / Cosmetic / Plastic Surgery Center, 2nd Floor, Daliganj Railway Crossing, 474A/170, Sitapur Road, Braham Nagar, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh – 226020. For any privacy question, please call +91 79065 57131.

2. Information we collect

We only ask for what is needed to respond to your enquiry:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number
  • Your email address, if you choose to provide it
  • The treatment area you are interested in, your preferred contact time and your message

Please do not send medical reports, prescriptions, identity documents or clinical photographs through the website enquiry form. If such material is needed, the clinic will tell you how to share it securely.

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you about the consultation you requested
  • To arrange or reschedule appointments
  • To answer general questions about the clinic and its services
  • To maintain internal records of enquiries for continuity of care

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. Consent and your choices

The enquiry form asks you to confirm that you agree to be contacted. You may withdraw that consent at any time by calling the clinic and asking us to stop contacting you. Withdrawing consent does not affect any care already provided.

5. How enquiries are transmitted

When you submit the enquiry form, your details are handed to WhatsApp so that you can send the message to the clinic from your own account. This means the message is delivered through WhatsApp's service as well as being received by us. If you prefer not to use WhatsApp, please call the clinic directly.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If analytics tools (for example Google Analytics 4) are enabled in future, they will be used to understand aggregate website usage only, and any non-essential cookies will be subject to your consent. Our embedded Google Map is provided by Google and may set its own cookies when the map loads; please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

7. Photography and patient images

Clinical photographs are published only with appropriate patient consent, and identifiable images are never shared without that consent. Before-and-after photographs are not retouched or altered.

8. How long we keep information

Enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable follow-up, after which they are removed. Clinical records relating to treatment are maintained separately as required by applicable medical record-keeping requirements.

9. Sharing

Information is accessed by the clinic team for the purposes above. We do not share your enquiry details with third parties for their own marketing. We may disclose information where required by law or by a lawful request from a competent authority.

10. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiry information is limited to those who need it to respond. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive medical details through the form.

11. Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete enquiry information that we are not required to keep. To make a request, call the clinic on +91 79065 57131.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Medical information is not advice. Nothing on this website creates a doctor–patient relationship. Please read our Medical Disclaimer.
