Privacy Policy

How PLASTICOS collects, uses and protects information provided through this website.

Last updated: 2025

PLASTICOS ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to provide information about our hair transplant, cosmetic and plastic surgery consultation services in Lucknow. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information you provide through this website.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website:

  • Contact details: Name, mobile number and email address (where provided) submitted through the enquiry form.
  • Enquiry details: The treatment you are interested in, your preferred contact time and the message you provide.
  • Consent records: Your consent to be contacted regarding your enquiry.
  • Technical information: Basic technical data such as browser type and device information, where applicable and permitted.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive medical information through this website. Please do not share sensitive medical details through the contact form. Such information should be discussed during an in-person consultation.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and schedule a consultation.
  • Communicate with you regarding your appointment or treatment enquiry.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you submit through this website. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We use spam protection on our contact form and recommend that you avoid sharing sensitive medical information through online forms.

Cookies

This website may use essential cookies for basic functionality. Where analytics or other non-essential cookies are used, consent will be sought in accordance with applicable law. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

This website embeds Google Maps for location display and may use third-party analytics services after appropriate setup. These third parties have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
