Liposuction in Lucknow – Body Contouring Consultation

Liposuction is a surgical procedure used for body contouring by removing selected localised fat deposits. A consultation helps determine whether the procedure is appropriate for your health, anatomy and goals.

Liposuction overview

Liposuction is a surgical body-contouring procedure. It is designed to reduce localised pockets of fat that have not responded to diet and physical activity, and to refine the shape of areas such as the abdomen, flanks or thighs.

It is not a weight-loss procedure and it is not a treatment for obesity. The best outcomes are usually seen in people near a stable weight, with good skin tone and realistic expectations about contour rather than numbers on a scale.

Important: liposuction changes shape, not weight. Fat can still return elsewhere if weight increases after the procedure.

Who may be suitable

  • Localised fat deposits that persist despite a stable diet and activity routine
  • Reasonably good skin elasticity, so that skin can adapt after fat removal
  • General health that allows surgery, without uncontrolled medical conditions
  • A stable weight, and no plans for significant further weight loss
  • Realistic expectations about the degree of change possible

Your assessment includes a review of your medical history, medicines, previous surgeries and any conditions affecting healing or clotting.

Consultation and assessment

At consultation, the surgeon examines the areas of concern, assesses skin quality and discusses what can realistically be achieved. You will be told about the type of anaesthesia involved, whether the procedure may be combined with others, the expected downtime and the risks specific to your case.

Photographs may be taken for your records with your consent. Measurements and planning are individual, and no two plans are identical.

Procedure overview

Liposuction is performed under anaesthesia appropriate to the planned procedure, which your surgeon will explain. Fat is removed through small incisions using a cannula. The technique, the number of areas treated and whether other procedures are combined are decided before surgery, after assessment.

Recovery

  • Swelling, bruising and soreness are expected in the first days to weeks.
  • A compression garment is usually worn for a period, as instructed.
  • Desk work can often be resumed within a short time; strenuous activity is restricted for longer.
  • Contour changes settle gradually over weeks and months as swelling resolves.

Aftercare instructions — including garment use, wound care, activity and follow-up appointments — are given in writing and should be followed exactly.

Possible risks and limitations

  • Bleeding, infection, fluid collection or delayed healing
  • Changes in skin sensation, usually temporary
  • Irregularities, asymmetry or loose skin after fat removal
  • Scarring at cannula entry points
  • Rare but serious complications associated with surgery and anaesthesia
  • Results that do not match expectations, sometimes requiring further procedures
