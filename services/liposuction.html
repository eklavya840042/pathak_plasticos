Liposuction in Lucknow – Body Contouring Consultation

Liposuction is a surgical procedure used for body contouring by removing selected localised fat deposits. A consultation helps determine whether the procedure is appropriate for your health, anatomy and goals.

Introduction

Liposuction is a surgical body-contouring procedure that removes localised fat deposits that have not responded adequately to diet and exercise. It is not a weight-loss procedure, nor a treatment for obesity. The procedure is generally considered by individuals who are near their target weight but have specific areas of persistent fat. Common areas treated include the abdomen, flanks, thighs, hips, upper arms, back and beneath the chin. Liposuction carries surgical risks and requires individual assessment of suitability, expected outcomes and recovery.

Who May Be Suitable

Suitability for liposuction is assessed individually. Candidates may include individuals who:

  • Are near their stable target weight with localised fat deposits.
  • Have good skin elasticity in the treatment area.
  • Are in good general health without conditions that increase surgical risk.
  • Have realistic expectations about what liposuction can achieve.
  • Do not smoke, or are willing to stop smoking before and after the procedure.

Consultation and Assessment Process

Your consultation with Dr. Brajesh Pathak includes a review of your medical history, current medications, previous surgeries and lifestyle factors. The areas of concern are examined, and the suitability of liposuction for your anatomy, skin condition and goals is discussed. Dr. Pathak explains the technique, anaesthesia considerations, expected outcomes, recovery and potential risks. If liposuction is appropriate, a treatment plan is prepared. If the procedure is not suitable, alternative options are discussed.

Procedure Overview

Where liposuction is undertaken, the procedure is performed under appropriate anaesthesia. Small incisions are made near the treatment area, a sterile solution is typically introduced, and a thin cannula is used to loosen and remove excess fat. The technique and approach may vary depending on the area treated, the volume of fat to be removed and individual factors. Specific procedural details, anaesthesia and facility considerations are explained during your consultation.

Recovery Information

Following the procedure, swelling, bruising and mild discomfort in the treated areas are common and typically settle over the following weeks. Compression garments are usually recommended for a period to support healing. Detailed post-procedure care instructions are provided, including guidance on activity, wound care and follow-up appointments. Most individuals can resume routine activities within a week or two, with specific restrictions explained during the consultation. Final results become apparent as swelling resolves, typically over several months. Recovery timelines vary between individuals.

Possible Risks and Limitations

As with any surgical procedure, liposuction carries potential risks, which may include:

  • Bleeding, infection or fluid accumulation.
  • Adverse reaction to anaesthesia.
  • Irregular contours, asymmetry or skin laxity in the treated area.
  • Changes in skin sensation, usually temporary.
  • Skin discolouration or prolonged swelling.
  • Need for additional procedures to refine results.
  • More serious complications, although uncommon, are discussed during consultation.
