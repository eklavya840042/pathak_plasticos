Breast Surgery Consultation in Lucknow

Explore breast surgery options through a personalised consultation. Depending on the clinic's available services, this may include breast augmentation and implant-related procedures. Treatment options and suitability are assessed individually.

Introduction

Breast surgery encompasses a range of procedures that may be considered for cosmetic or reconstructive reasons. Depending on the services offered by the clinic at the time of consultation, breast surgery options may include breast augmentation, which uses implants to increase breast size or restore volume. Each procedure carries its own considerations regarding suitability, expected outcomes, recovery and risks. A consultation is essential to understand which, if any, procedure is appropriate for your individual anatomy, health and goals.

Who May Be Suitable

Breast surgery consultation may be appropriate for individuals who:

  • Have specific concerns about breast size, shape or volume.
  • Are in good general health without conditions that increase surgical risk.
  • Have realistic expectations about achievable outcomes.
  • Are not currently pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Do not smoke, or are willing to stop smoking before and after any procedure.

Consultation and Assessment Process

Your consultation with Dr. Brajesh Pathak includes a review of your medical history, current medications, breast health and any relevant family history. The specific concerns you have are discussed, and an examination is conducted. Dr. Pathak explains the procedures offered by the clinic at the time of consultation, the suitability of each for your anatomy and goals, the expected outcomes, recovery and risks. If a procedure is appropriate, a treatment plan is discussed. If breast surgery is not suitable for you, this is explained.

Procedure Overview

Where breast surgery is undertaken, the specific procedure depends on the individual plan. For breast augmentation, implants are typically placed through carefully planned incisions, with the approach, implant type and placement discussed during consultation. Each procedure has its own technical considerations, anaesthesia requirements and facility implications. Specific procedural details are explained by Dr. Brajesh Pathak based on your individual treatment plan.

Recovery Information

Recovery after breast surgery depends on the specific procedure performed. Swelling, bruising and discomfort are common and typically settle over the following weeks. Supportive garments are usually recommended, and detailed post-procedure care instructions are provided, including activity restrictions, wound care and follow-up appointments. Most individuals can resume routine activities within a few weeks, with specific restrictions explained during the consultation. Recovery timelines vary between individuals and depend on the procedure performed.

Possible Risks and Limitations

As with any surgical procedure, breast surgery carries potential risks, which may include:

  • Bleeding, infection or delayed healing.
  • Adverse reaction to anaesthesia.
  • Changes in nipple or breast sensation, which may be temporary or prolonged.
  • Visible scarring, though typically placed to be discreet.
  • Asymmetry, irregular contours or suboptimal outcome.
  • For implant-based procedures: implant-related considerations such as capsular contracture, implant displacement or future implant replacement.
  • Need for additional procedures in selected cases.
