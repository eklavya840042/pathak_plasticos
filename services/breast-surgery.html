Breast surgery consultation overview

Breast surgery covers a range of procedures with different aims — changing size or shape, restoring volume after pregnancy or weight change, or reducing discomfort associated with breast size. Which procedure, if any, is appropriate depends on your anatomy, health, history and expectations.

This page describes consultation in general terms. The specific procedures offered by the clinic are confirmed when you speak with the team, because availability and suitability vary.

Please confirm availability: not every breast procedure is offered at every clinic. Ask the team which breast procedures are currently available at PLASTICOS during your enquiry.

Procedures that may be discussed

  • Breast augmentation, including implant-based options where offered
  • Breast lift (mastopexy), where drooping rather than volume is the main concern
  • Breast reduction, where size is associated with symptoms such as back or shoulder discomfort
  • Revision or implant-related consultation, where relevant

Who may be suitable

  • Adults with a stable weight and completed breast development
  • General health that allows surgery, without uncontrolled medical conditions
  • Clear, realistic expectations about size, shape, scarring and recovery
  • No active breast infection or unresolved breast condition
  • Willingness to attend follow-up appointments and follow aftercare

Consultation and assessment

Your consultation includes a discussion of your goals and concerns, your medical and family history, previous pregnancies and any breast symptoms. Examination and measurements help identify which options are realistic. Where a breast symptom needs investigation, that is arranged before any cosmetic surgery is considered.

Implant type, size, placement and the limitations of each option are discussed in detail, including the need for long-term follow-up and the possibility of further surgery over time.

Recovery

  • Swelling, tightness and discomfort are expected initially.
  • Support garments and restricted movement may be advised for a period.
  • Desk work is often possible within a short time; strenuous activity and heavy lifting are restricted for longer.
  • Settling of shape continues over weeks and months.

Possible risks and limitations

  • Bleeding, infection, delayed healing or fluid collection
  • Scarring, and changes in nipple or breast sensation
  • Asymmetry, capsular contracture or implant-related complications where implants are used
  • Need for revision surgery over time
  • Impact on breastfeeding and on future screening discussions, which should be discussed individually
