PRP Hair Treatment in Lucknow

PRP, or Platelet-Rich Plasma treatment, is a procedure that uses a preparation derived from the patient's blood. Consult the clinic to understand its suitability, potential benefits, limitations and recommended treatment plan.

Introduction

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) is a preparation produced from a small sample of the patient's own blood. The blood is processed to separate and concentrate the platelets, which are then prepared for application. PRP is discussed in the context of certain hair concerns, and its use is sometimes considered alongside or instead of other options. As with any procedure, the evidence for its effectiveness varies across conditions and individuals, and a consultation is necessary to understand whether it is appropriate for your specific concerns.

Who May Be Suitable

PRP may be discussed as an option for individuals who:

  • Are experiencing early or mild pattern hair loss and seek non-surgical options.
  • Wish to explore adjunctive treatments alongside other hair restoration approaches.
  • Are considered suitable following individual clinical assessment.
  • Have realistic expectations about potential benefits and limitations.
  • Do not have contraindications identified during consultation.

Consultation and Assessment Process

Your consultation with Dr. Brajesh Pathak includes a review of your hair concerns, medical history, current medications and previous treatments. Dr. Pathak explains what PRP is, how it is prepared and applied, the current evidence regarding its use for your specific concern, and its potential benefits and limitations. If PRP is considered appropriate, a suggested treatment plan including number of sessions, intervals and expectations is discussed. If PRP is not suitable, alternative options are explained.

Procedure Overview

Where PRP is undertaken, a small sample of blood is drawn and processed to prepare the platelet-rich plasma. The prepared PRP is then applied to the treatment area. The procedure is typically performed in an outpatient setting. Specific details regarding preparation method, application technique, session frequency and the areas to be treated are explained during your consultation, as approaches may vary.

Recovery Information

PRP is generally associated with minimal downtime. Mild tenderness, redness or swelling at the treatment site may occur and typically settles within a short period. Specific post-treatment care instructions are provided, including guidance on activity, hair washing and any restrictions. Multiple sessions are often discussed as part of a treatment plan, with intervals explained during consultation. Recovery experiences vary between individuals.

Possible Risks and Limitations

PRP uses the patient's own blood, but as with any procedure, potential considerations include:

  • Mild pain, tenderness, redness or swelling at the treatment or blood-draw sites.
  • Bruising at the blood-draw site.
  • Risk of infection, although low when performed under appropriate conditions.
  • Variable or suboptimal response — outcomes differ between individuals.
  • Need for multiple sessions to assess response.
  • PRP may not be suitable as a standalone option for advanced hair loss.
