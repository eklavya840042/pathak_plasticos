PRP Hair Treatment in Lucknow

PRP, or Platelet-Rich Plasma treatment, is a procedure that uses a preparation derived from the patient’s blood. Consult the clinic to understand its suitability, potential benefits, limitations and recommended treatment plan.

What PRP treatment is

Platelet-Rich Plasma is a preparation made by taking a small sample of your own blood and processing it to concentrate the platelet-rich portion of the plasma. That preparation is then used in a treatment plan for selected hair concerns, depending on the assessment.

Because the preparation comes from your own blood, the material itself is not foreign to your body. That does not mean PRP is risk-free or that it works for everyone, and the evidence for its use in hair loss is mixed. Any discussion of benefit should include what is not yet known.

Who may be considered for PRP

  • Selected cases of pattern hair loss or thinning, usually as part of a broader plan
  • Patients looking for non-surgical options alongside medical management
  • Some patients preparing for, or following, a hair transplant — where the surgeon considers it appropriate

PRP is not a treatment for every type of hair loss. If your hair loss has a hormonal, nutritional, inflammatory or other medical cause, that needs to be addressed first.

Consultation and assessment

Your consultation begins with understanding your hair loss history, general health and any medicines or supplements you take. Blood disorders, active infection, certain medications and some medical conditions may mean PRP is not appropriate. Suitability and the number of sessions, if any, are decided after assessment — not before.

What a session involves

Where PRP is planned, a small blood sample is collected, processed to prepare the plasma, and then used in the treatment area. Sessions are relatively short and are usually performed as an outpatient visit. The exact number and spacing of sessions is planned individually and reviewed as you go.

After a session

  • The treated area may feel tender or slightly swollen for a short time.
  • You may be advised to avoid washing your hair, strenuous exercise or certain products for a brief period.
  • Any change in hair appearance is gradual, and results differ between individuals.

Benefits, limitations and risks

Some patients report improvement in hair quality or density, and others see little change. PRP should not be presented as a guaranteed or permanent solution. Possible issues include discomfort at the injection or treatment site, temporary swelling or bruising, and infection at the site (uncommon). The overall evidence is not strong enough to promise a particular outcome.
