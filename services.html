How consultations work

Assessment before recommendation

Every treatment at PLASTICOS follows the same starting point: understanding your concern, your medical history and what you hope to achieve. Only then are options discussed.

  • Medical history review — including previous treatments, medication and relevant conditions.
  • Clinical assessment — examination of the area of concern and suitability factors.
  • Options discussion — realistic outcomes, limitations, recovery and risks.
  • Written plan — what is recommended, what it involves and what to expect.
