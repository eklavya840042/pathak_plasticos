Our approach

The clinic

A centre for hair restoration and plastic surgery consultations

PLASTICOS was set up to offer focused, unhurried consultations for people concerned about hair loss, facial hair density and selected cosmetic or plastic surgery procedures.

Treatment categories at the clinic cover hair restoration (including hair transplant, beard transplant and PRP), body contouring and select cosmetic surgery procedures. Each enquiry begins with an assessment of your concerns, medical history and expectations.

Patients travel to the centre from across Lucknow and nearby districts. The clinic is located on the 2nd floor at Daliganj Railway Crossing on Sitapur Road, with consultation visits arranged by prior appointment.

  • Hair restoration: hair transplant, beard transplant, PRP consultations.
  • Cosmetic and plastic surgery: liposuction, gynecomastia, mommy makeover and breast surgery consultations.
  • Every recommendation is made after an individual assessment — not before.

Clinic philosophy

Informed Decisions. Personalised Care.

We believe patients should understand their treatment options, possible risks, recovery expectations and limitations before making a decision.

Listen first

Your concerns, history and expectations are discussed before any procedure is proposed.

Assess suitability

Not every concern needs a procedure. Suitability is assessed individually and alternatives are discussed.

Explain honestly

Expected outcomes, limitations, downtime and risks are explained in plain language.

Plan together

Treatment planning is a conversation. You are encouraged to ask questions and take your time.

Your first visit

What to expect at your consultation

A consultation at PLASTICOS is a conversation, an assessment and a written plan — in that order.

Step 01

Discuss your concerns

Share what bothers you, how long it has been present, any previous treatments and your goals.

Step 02

Clinical assessment

Relevant examination, medical history and, where needed, investigations to understand suitability.

Step 03

Options and expectations

Possible approaches, what each can and cannot achieve, recovery and risks are explained.

Step 04

Your decision

You decide in your own time. There is no obligation to proceed after a consultation.

Have a question before booking?

Speak with the PLASTICOS team about appointment availability, what a consultation involves, or which treatment page applies to your concern.
