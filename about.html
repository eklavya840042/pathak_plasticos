The Clinic

About PLASTICOS

PLASTICOS is an advanced hair transplant, cosmetic and plastic surgery centre located on Sitapur Road, Braham Nagar, Lucknow. The clinic provides a dedicated space for consultation, treatment planning and selected procedures across hair restoration and cosmetic surgery.

The clinic is led by Dr. Brajesh Pathak, M.Ch Plastic Surgeon, who is associated with PLASTICOS and personally involved in patient consultations. The clinic's approach emphasises understanding individual concerns, explaining available options and recommending treatments only after a thorough assessment of suitability, expectations and recovery considerations.

Treatment Categories

  • Hair restoration consultations — including hair transplant and beard transplant.
  • Non-surgical hair treatment options — such as PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) consultation.
  • Body contouring procedures — including liposuction and gynecomastia surgery consultations.
  • Post-pregnancy aesthetic consultations — mommy makeover planning.
  • Breast surgery consultations — assessed on individual suitability.

Consultation Approach

Every consultation begins with a discussion of your concerns, medical history and expectations. Dr. Brajesh Pathak explains the available treatment options, what each procedure does and does not do, and what recovery typically involves. Treatment planning is individualised, and recommendations are made only when a procedure is considered suitable for your anatomy, health and goals.

Our Philosophy

Informed Decisions. Personalised Care.

We believe patients should understand their treatment options, possible risks, recovery expectations and limitations before making a decision.

Understanding Before Recommendation

We take the time to understand your concerns and goals before suggesting any procedure. No two patients have identical anatomy or expectations.

Transparent About Limitations

Every procedure has limitations. We communicate what a treatment can realistically achieve and where it may not be the right fit for you.

Education and Communication

Patients receive clear information about procedures, recovery timelines and post-treatment care so that decisions are made with realistic expectations.

Respect for Individual Goals

Your treatment plan is built around your individual needs and circumstances — not a template. Recommendations respect your medical history and expectations.
