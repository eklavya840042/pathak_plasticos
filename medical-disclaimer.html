General information only

The content on this website — including treatment descriptions, frequently asked questions and recovery information — is provided for general awareness. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a treatment recommendation for any individual.

No doctor–patient relationship

Reading this website, submitting an enquiry form, calling the clinic or messaging on WhatsApp does not create a doctor–patient relationship. That relationship begins only after a consultation and clinical assessment with Dr. Brajesh Pathak at PLASTICOS.

Individual assessment is required

Every procedure described here requires an in-person assessment of your health, medical history, anatomy and expectations. Suitability cannot be determined from a website, a phone call or a message. Any treatment plan is made only after consultation.

Results are not guaranteed

Outcomes of hair restoration, cosmetic and plastic surgery procedures vary from person to person. No procedure can be guaranteed to produce a particular result, and no specific outcome should be expected on the basis of this website or of photographs.

Risks and recovery

All surgical procedures carry risks, including bleeding, infection, scarring, altered sensation, asymmetry, dissatisfaction with appearance and, in some cases, the need for further procedures. Recovery times vary. Your surgeon will discuss the risks relevant to your procedure before you decide.

Patient images

Any clinical photographs are published only with appropriate consent, are not retouched, and are not a promise of what you can expect. Where representative or illustrative images are used, they are labelled as such.

Information on this website does not replace the instructions of your treating doctor. Do not start, stop or change any medicine on the basis of anything published here.

Emergencies

This website and its contact channels are not for emergencies. If you have severe pain, breathing difficulty, heavy bleeding, signs of infection, or any other urgent medical problem, contact your nearest hospital or emergency service immediately.

Treatment descriptions are reviewed by the clinic. Medical knowledge changes, and details such as technique, aftercare and availability may be updated without notice. If something on this website appears unclear or out of date, please confirm the current position with the clinic.
