Questions About Hair Transplant & Cosmetic Surgery

General answers about consultations, treatments and recovery at PLASTICOS, Lucknow. These are for information only — your own suitability is determined in consultation.

Hair restoration

Hair transplant & PRP

Suitability depends on the pattern and stability of hair loss, the quality and density of donor hair at the back and sides of the scalp, your general health and your expectations. A consultation with an examination of the scalp is needed before any recommendation can be made.

The consultation covers your history of hair loss, previous treatments, medical background, donor-area assessment, the technique that may be appropriate, the number of sessions that may be involved, recovery and the limitations of the procedure.

Most people can return to desk work within a few days, but timelines vary. Scabbing settles over the first week or two, and transplanted hair commonly sheds before new growth appears. Visible improvement is usually judged over months rather than weeks. Your surgeon will give you instructions specific to your procedure.

Transplanted hair is generally taken from donor areas that are more resistant to hair loss, but the remaining natural hair can continue to thin over time. Ongoing medical management may be discussed as part of a longer-term plan. No procedure can guarantee a permanent result for every patient.

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) uses a preparation made from your own blood, which is prepared in a laboratory and then used as part of a treatment plan in selected cases. It is not a substitute for a hair transplant and its role, benefits and limitations should be discussed individually. Evidence for PRP in hair loss is mixed, and results vary.
